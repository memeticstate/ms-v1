# Compass Route — Memetic State identity

Memetic State’s compass marks a field of observation: four survey corners define the view, cardinal axes orient it, diagonal spokes converge at a cream center, and a dotted quarter-circle connects two observations. The mark represents navigation, interpretation and relationships.

Use the current vector masters and palette defined in `brand-guidelines.md`. The former curved-path and terminal-diamond construction is retired. Recoloring that former construction does not make it an approved logo. New artwork must start from the compass master, never an older export or conversation image.

The visual language remains warm and archival: cream, amber, sage, rust and rose on near-black. The wordmark pairs bold uppercase MEMETIC with an italic serif State. Preserve the compass geometry and avoid fluorescent colors, glow or glossy treatments.
