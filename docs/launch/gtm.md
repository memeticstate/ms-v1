# Memetic State launch operating plan

## The wedge

Own one question:

> What is actually happening across PONS launches on Robinhood Chain, and which activity deserves a closer look?

Memetic State answers with canonical evidence. X distributes the answer. Every public observation should link back to the exact state, dossier, or evidence surface that supports it.

## The 30-day loop

The initial GTM is one repeatable loop—not four parallel campaigns:

1. **Observe:** publish one evidence-backed PONS observation every day.
2. **Return:** give researchers a reason to come back through saved launches and useful alerts.
3. **Learn:** recruit ten design partners from the people who repeatedly inspect or share the work.
4. **Convert:** offer a paid workflow only after one behavior becomes habitual.

PONS remains the intelligence universe. NVDA remains the focused flagship habitat. The category launches before any token.

## Now — days 0–7: establish the signal

Ship:

- Reliable live collection with visible lag, reconciliation, and metadata coverage.
- A daily PONS Pulse with copyable text and an X-ready visual card.
- Stable links to filtered signals, pair habitats, and launch dossiers.
- A public methodology and compact product thesis.

Publish:

- Day 1: category statement and pinned thesis thread.
- Daily: one verified State Pulse or unusual launch observation.
- Twice in the week: one Launch Autopsy or Habitat Shift.
- Once in the week: one Evidence Check, including degraded status when relevant.

Recruit:

- Four active PONS traders or researchers.
- Three launch teams.
- Three ecosystem, wallet, explorer, or data builders.

Gate: collection stays dependable and at least five design partners use the product twice during the week.

## Next — days 8–21: create return behavior

Ship:

- Saved launches and stock habitats.
- Configurable signal, activity, lifecycle, and integrity alerts.
- Shareable research cards and compact launch briefs.
- A lightweight changelog showing what the engine learned or improved.

Operate:

- Review saved rules and alert opens with design partners each week.
- Ask only workflow questions: what did they monitor, what made them return, and what did they still check manually?
- Turn recurring manual research into one reliable product action.

Gate: 25 weekly researchers, seven returning in consecutive weeks, and five unprompted requests for alerts, history, exports, or API access.

## Then — days 22–30: test willingness to pay

Offer two manual founding products:

- **Founding Researcher:** longer comparisons, more alert capacity, export packs, and direct research sessions.
- **Team Pilot:** API/webhooks, bulk history, launch monitoring, and data-quality status.

Sell through short workflow calls, not a large pricing page. Price saved monitoring and research time—not token appreciation.

Gate: five paid researchers or two paid team pilots. If neither happens, improve the workflow before expanding utility.

## Content operating system

| Format | Cadence | Evidence destination |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| State Pulse | Daily | Signals |
| Launch Autopsy | 2× weekly | Launch dossier |
| Habitat Shift | Weekly | Atlas/cohort |
| Evidence Check | Weekly | Evidence |
| Build Log | Weekly | Network/changelog |

The account behaves like a sharp research desk: one falsifiable observation per post, restrained language, and a direct path to the underlying evidence.

## Scoreboard

North-star: **weekly evidence-backed decisions**—sessions where someone inspects a signal and opens, saves, exports, or shares its underlying evidence.

Track only:

- Weekly returning researchers.
- Signal → dossier inspection.
- Saved launches and active alert rules.
- Shared-view opens and visual/text exports.
- Design-partner repeat use.
- Founding membership or team-pilot conversion.
- Collection success, index lag, reconciliation, and metadata coverage.

Do not optimize impressions in isolation.

## Utility boundary

The product must become useful without a token. Start with founding identity and ordinary product access. Token-dependent utility remains inactive until the reliability, habit, demand, payment, contribution, and legal gates in `supporter-utility.md` pass.
