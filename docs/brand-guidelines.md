# Memetic State — Field Archive identity

## Brand idea

Memetic State is a field atlas for a market that is still forming. The identity combines the discipline of an archive with the material warmth of objects handled by people: green-black ink, bone paper, lichen, oxidized clay, aged brass and river-worn slate. It must feel exact without feeling synthetic.

## Logo logic

The mark combines four survey corners, an irregular route, observation nodes and a terminal diamond. **Aged Brass** frames the field, **Lichen Green** traces verified movement, **Oxidized Clay** marks cultural observations, and a short **Bone Paper** branch represents human interpretation. These roles stay consistent in the interface.

Use `logo-mark.svg` for avatars, favicons and square placements. Use `logo-lockup.svg` for headers and partner material. Preserve clear space equal to one corner-arm length. Do not add glow, glossy gradients, drop shadows or fluorescent colors.

## UI allocation

Dark mode is the night field station; light mode is the working notebook in daylight. Both use the same semantic hierarchy. Lichen/Moss means verified or constructive change. Brass means attention or interpretation. Clay/Ochre means cultural state, cooling, historical work or warning. Red is reserved for actual failure. Color is always paired with a label, icon or value.

Surfaces should resemble stacked paper, boards and instrument plates rather than glowing glass. Gradients may suggest ambient light or topography but must remain nearly invisible. The system avoids neon cyan, digital violet, pure black and pure white.

## Typography

Use the compact sans for navigation and explanations, IBM Plex Mono or the system monospace stack for evidence, and the restrained old-style serif for the word “State” and a small number of editorial thesis lines. Data tables never use the serif.
