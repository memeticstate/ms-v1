Memetic State

A field guide to culture on Robinhood Chain

Memetic State helps you explore what is forming, persisting, changing, and fading across Robinhood Chain. It brings launches, pools, activity, and relationships into a shared map, then asks what the evidence can tell us about them.

Energy flows to what is alive.

Here, “alive” means that participation is beginning to persist and organize. A busy chart alone does not establish that. The useful question is what supports a reading, what is missing, and what would change our minds.

This book explains the website, the intended role of the token, and how the project plans to fund further development.

What is the token for?

The token is intended to connect access and participation across Memetic State: deeper research through the premium tier, and .

The economic direction is to reinvest a portion of creator fees actually received into the project and token buybacks. Both are being developed; neither guarantees a token price outcome.
