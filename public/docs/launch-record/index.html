These initial entries reflect the founder's account. Transaction references, wallet addresses, and expense evidence still need to be attached. They are not presented as independently reconciled onchain balances.

Entry Reported fact Accounting treatment
Initial dev-wallet purchase 2.66% of token supply purchased at launch Historical founder holding; current balance may differ.
Earlier creator-fee claim 1.75 NVDA claimed with the stated purpose of paying for a Dexscreener banner Historical claim intended for marketing; exact spending and any remainder require reconciliation.
Future creator-fee program Reinvestment and buybacks are intended Proposed policy; no fixed split or completed action is asserted.

The 1.75 NVDA is the reported claim amount, not a verified banner invoice amount or an assumed USD cost. Any unspent balance remains to be identified. It is not counted as either a completed buyback or automatically available funding for the new program.

The future program will have a recorded effective date. The historical banner claim will remain visible as its own entry; it will not be relabeled to suggest all claims since launch funded buybacks.

The founder may later sell initial holdings for income or development. No lock or no-sale promise has been made. Founder sales, treasury contributions, and creator-fee claims are separate entries. This disclosure does not assert that any founder sale has occurred.
