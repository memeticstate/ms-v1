Reading the evidence

Memetic State studies organization over time. Activity provides a starting point; context determines how much weight it deserves.

The evidence behind a reading

Dimension Question
Activity What participation is observable?
Persistence Does it continue beyond the initial burst?
Breadth How concentrated is it across wallets and repeated participants?
Liquidity Is market structure holding up or weakening?
Relationships Do pairings or cross-venue appearances recur?
Change How does the current period differ from its baseline?
Provenance Can the reading be traced to an identifiable source and time window?

No single measurement proves durable interest. Wallet counts can overstate the number of people involved, repeated trades can inflate activity, and a thin pool can make small trades look dramatic.

Observation, calculation, interpretation

An observation records something from a source, such as a swap or pool creation.

A calculation combines observations, such as a change in activity over a defined window.

An interpretation proposes what that pattern might mean, such as formation, stress, or fading.

Those levels carry different uncertainty. A readable explanation should let you work backward toward its supporting records. AI-generated language does not add evidence by itself.

What would change the reading?

A useful research note includes an invalidation condition. A claim of persistent participation becomes weaker if later activity disappears, remains highly concentrated, or depends on a collection error. An apparent relationship becomes less persuasive if it is simply the default configuration of a launch venue.

“Investigate next” identifies the check that could resolve that uncertainty: compare another period, inspect concentration, verify a source record, or look for continued activity after a transition.

Learning from outcomes

Historical cases help evaluate whether a description was justified by the evidence available at the time. Later success does not make an unsupported original claim sound, and later failure does not erase a real earlier observation.

The methodology will evolve as coverage and replay improve. Changes to material definitions should be documented so earlier labels remain understandable.
