Access and token

Token purpose and the flywheel

Will the token have utility and a flywheel?

The direction is both product utility and a fee-supported economic loop, alongside the social loop created by people using and sharing the work.

The token's first product role is access to deeper research through Premium Field Operator at the 0.1% holder threshold.

On the funding side, the project intends to reinvest a portion of creator fees actually received into development and open-market token buybacks. The treasury and reporting process are being designed. There is no live automated compounding or permanent allocation claim in this plan.

The economic loop

Eligible trading can generate creator fees. Received fees can fund useful work and token purchases. Better tools and experiences can give people reasons to participate again.

This is the proposed money flow. Website visits and token holding do not automatically generate fees.

Buybacks create actual purchases when executed. A separate, verifiable burn can remove acquired tokens from usable circulation. Neither action guarantees that demand exceeds selling or that price rises.

The social loop

Research gives people things to explore and discuss.

That loop is useful only if people value the experience. It can develop even during periods of low token trading, which is why participation and revenue need to be measured separately.

What we intend to make accountable

The proposed program will report received fees, project spending, reserved funds, and completed buybacks. It will also distinguish any burns or locks from purchases alone.

There is no promise of a price floor, an always-growing treasury, or a self-sustaining business before income and costs demonstrate one. The aim is useful products with an understandable funding path.
