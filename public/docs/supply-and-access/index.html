Supply, holdings, and access

The token has launched. This documentation does not create a new distribution, reserve, vesting schedule, or team allocation.

The project-supplied token contract is:

0xeDf1AC25ff0CF741A9eE1490C630E3a4eCfBd4FC

This is the token contract, not the founder's wallet or a treasury address. Verify the chain and contract against the project's official launch information before interacting. Total supply, decimals, permissions, liquidity terms, and any locks must be established from the actual launch and contracts; no unverified values are asserted here.

Founder holding

The founder reports purchasing 2.66% of supply through the dev wallet at launch. That is a historical purchase, not a reserved team allocation or a verified current balance.

Those tokens are not represented as locked or subject to a promised vesting schedule. The founder may sell some or all of this holding for personal income or development. A sale can create market pressure. Its proceeds are separate from creator fees unless a specific contribution to the project is disclosed.
