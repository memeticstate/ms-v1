Glossary

Term Meaning here
Creator fees Fees attributable to the creator under the relevant platform's rules; the treasury model counts amounts actually received.
Buyback A purchase of the project's token on the open market using project funds.
Burn A verifiable disposition that permanently removes tokens from usable circulation; the exact method and supply effect must be disclosed.
Lock A restriction on access or transfer with stated terms; it is not necessarily permanent.
Treasury or fee vault The proposed account or infrastructure for receiving and managing project revenue.
Reinvestment Using received income to fund further activity.
Compounding Reinvesting earned returns into an income-generating base; merely accumulating deposits does not establish it.
Economic flywheel A proposed feedback loop connecting income, funding, utility, and further participation.
Social flywheel A loop of participation, outputs, sharing, and renewed interest.
Evidence window The source records and time period supporting a displayed observation or interpretation.
Supply reference The explicit quantity used to calculate an access threshold.
